## Creative direction

- Contemporary luxury fashion house; minimalist, architectural, editorial, sophisticated and premium.
- Confident femininity: elegant and strong, with generous whitespace, intentional composition and restrained typography.
- Clothing and fashion photography take visual priority.
- African identity expressed through craftsmanship, collections and storytelling; avoid stereotypical patterns and generic fashion-store styling.

## Reference brands and sites recorded in the recap

- TOTEME: restraint, modern minimalism and visual discipline.
- The Row: quiet luxury, whitespace and understated sophistication.
- KHAITE: confident femininity, strong silhouettes and expressive imagery.
- Jil Sander: architectural simplicity, structure and refined typography.

Use these as inspiration for presentation, navigation, imagery, typography and shopping experience. Specific preferred sections have not been recorded in the accessible source.

## Colours and typography

- Core direction reported: white/off-white, heavenly pink and black.
- Wine/burgundy: possible fourth accent colour, not finalised.
- Use pink sparingly and keep the palette restrained.
- Playfair Display: reported existing brand typeface to carry into the website.
- Pair with a clean modern sans-serif for navigation, product information and interface text; secondary font undecided.
- Exact colour values have not been approved in the available record.

## Storefront experience

| Area | Recorded direction |
| --- | --- |
| Homepage | Large editorial fashion imagery, prominent brand presentation, curated collections and storytelling |
| Navigation | Clean, intuitive, minimal and easy to browse |
| Shop | Spacious product grids, strong photography and restrained product information |
| Product pages | Large images, clear sizes and colours, fabric details, size guides, pricing and availability |
| Recommendations | Refined “What Others Are Buying” or related-products section |
| Cart and checkout | Smooth, uncluttered, premium and low friction |
| Customer account | Consistent sign-in, wishlist, order history and tracking |
| Mobile | Equally polished, with easy navigation and usable shopping interactions |

Avoid excessive animation, unnecessary carousels, cluttered icons and aggressive discount banners.

## Working design statement

RIVERMINDSTYLE should feel like a refined international fashion house: editorial photography, architectural whitespace, Playfair Display typography, restrained off-white/pink/black branding, and a confident feminine identity with the clothing at the centre of attention.
